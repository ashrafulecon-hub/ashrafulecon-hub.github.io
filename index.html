01 / Background

About me

I am pursuing an MA in Economics at Central Michigan University, with a concentration in Econometrics and Quantitative Economics. My interests connect macroeconomics, public policy, and applied empirical research.

My research experience includes household surveys, data analysis, and collaborative projects on poverty, migration, water insecurity, and mental health in Bangladesh. I am interested in using economic evidence to inform policy and development.

02 / Research agenda

Current research interests & projects

Research proposal

Governors’ education and economic performance during crises

Examining the association between governors’ educational backgrounds and state economic outcomes, using state and year fixed effects.

Research project

Economic growth, renewable energy, and U.S. CO₂ emissions

Studying symmetric and asymmetric relationships with panel time-series methods and the environmental Kuznets curve framework.

Research project

Water insecurity, migration, and household poverty

Investigating environmental stress and household responses in coastal Bangladesh using survey data and applied microeconometric methods.

Research project

Socioeconomic and health factors in urban mental health

Analyzing anxiety and associated socioeconomic and health factors among respondents in Dhaka and Khulna.
